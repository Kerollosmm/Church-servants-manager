# Product Guidelines - Church Servants Manager (CSMS)

## Communication Style & Prose
*   **Direct & Instructional:** In-app text should be clear and concise, providing direct guidance on actions (e.g., "Tap to log attendance").
*   **Helpful & Encouraging:** Maintain a warm and supportive tone that provides context and encourages users in their service tasks.
*   **Clear Terminology:** Use consistent and precise language. Define key terms (e.g., "Servant", "Student", "Session") and use them strictly throughout the app.
*   **Context-Aware:** Use language that respects the spiritual and community context of the church while ensuring clarity for all users.

## Localization & Language (Arabic)
*   **Dialect & Tone:** Use **Simplified Modern Standard Arabic (Fos-ha Mobasata)** mixed with **Polite Egyptian** constraints. The goal is to sound natural and friendly, not robotic or overly formal.
*   **Terminology:**
    *   Use `تأكيد` (Confirm) instead of `إعتماد`.
    *   Use `حذف` (Delete) instead of `إزالة`.
    *   Use `رصد الغياب` (Log Attendance) instead of `تسجيل الحضور`.
*   **Avoid:** Complex classical terms (لغة مقعرة) or heavy street slang. Keep it respectful yet accessible for church servants.

## UI/UX Principles
*   **Material 3 Foundation:** Adhere to modern Material Design 3 standards to ensure a consistent, premium feel across Android, iOS, and Web.
*   **Accessibility First:** Prioritize high-contrast text, large and accessible touch targets, and comprehensive semantic labels for screen readers.
*   **Focused Workflows:** Design screens to support a single-task focus, guiding users through one clear action at a time to reduce cognitive load and errors.
*   **Scannable Data:** Use clear typography, distinct headers, and generous spacing to make data (like student lists and attendance records) easy to read at a glance.
*   **Logical Grouping:** Organize related information into visually distinct cards or sections.

## Feedback & State Management
*   **Instant Confirmation:** Provide immediate visual or haptic feedback for every successful action (e.g., Snackbars for "Attendance saved").
*   **Actionable Error Handling:** Use descriptive error messages that explain what happened and provide a clear path to resolution.
*   **Offline Awareness:** Clearly communicate the app's state when offline, reassuring users that their data is stored locally and will sync automatically.

## Visual Design & Branding
*   **Professional & Secure:** Maintain a clean aesthetic that emphasizes data integrity and trustworthiness.
*   **Modern & Minimalist:** Prioritize whitespace and high contrast to ensure the UI feels light, fast, and easy to navigate.
