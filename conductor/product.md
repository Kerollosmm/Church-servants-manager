# Product Guide - Church Servants Manager (CSMS)

## Initial Concept
A comprehensive Flutter application designed to help church servants and administrators manage student data and church activities efficiently.

## Target Users
*   **Church Administrators:** Users with full access to the system, responsible for managing servant assignments, student data, and overall church records.
*   **Church Servants:** Users responsible for specific student groups, focused on daily attendance tracking and service activities.
*   **Students/Parents:** Users who interact with the system to view schedules, receive updates, and stay connected with church families.

## Core Goals
*   **Centralization:** Transitioning from fragmented or paper-based tracking to a single, unified digital source of truth for all student and service data.
*   **Efficiency:** Automating attendance tracking and simplifying reporting for church leadership to save time and reduce manual errors.
*   **Communication:** Enhancing coordination and information sharing between administrators, servants, and church families.

## Key Features (MVP)
*   **Attendance Tracking:** Real-time logging of student attendance for sessions, activities, and special events.
*   **Student Directory:** A searchable and organized directory of student profiles, including contact information, spiritual history, and important notes.

## User Experience & Design Priorities
*   **Mobile-First Efficiency:** Prioritizing quick actions like one-tap attendance and rapid student search to support servants on the go.
*   **Visual Indicators:** Use of status icons and color-coding for attendance (e.g., present/absent/late).
*   **Robust Offline Support:** Leveraging local storage to ensure data can be logged without a network connection and synchronized later.

## Visual Aesthetic & Tone
*   **Professional & Trusted:** A clean, secure-feeling interface built on Material Design principles that emphasizes data integrity.
*   **Modern & Minimalist:** A high-contrast, simplified layout with ample whitespace to ensure focus and ease of use.

## Future Roadmap
*   **Activity Scheduler:** A centralized calendar for managing and viewing church sessions, trips, and special events, to be implemented after mastering the core directory and attendance features.
