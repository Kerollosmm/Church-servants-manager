# CSMS Product Context

## Product Identity
- **Name**: ChurchServers Management System (CSMS)
- **Register**: `product` (Internal admin & mobile management system for church servants & leaders)
- **Primary Audience**: Church Administrators, Servants (Helpers), Group Leaders & Teachers

## Purpose & Context of Use
CSMS is designed for church servants managing community members, student rosters, attendance tracking, and service reports. Users operate the system during active services, quiet administrative hours, and field/visitation contexts. Interfaces must be ultra-fast, scannable, offline-resilient, and visually calming.

## Creative North Star: "The Modern Sanctuary"
The visual identity marries the warmth of traditional heritage with the precision of modern digital tools. It rejects cold corporate SaaS conventions (standard blue, stark grays, noisy cards) in favor of a grounded, earthy, dignified atmosphere centered around warm Ochre (`#A58255`) and soft stone tones.

## Brand Tone & Values
- **Dignified & Warm**: Respectful, institutional yet inviting, grounded in community heritage.
- **Clear & Precise**: Low cognitive load, high scannability, dense data rendered with calm hierarchy.
- **Meditative Focus**: Generous whitespace, quiet surfaces, subtle ambient depth instead of harsh lines.
- **Bilingual & Inclusive**: Full RTL-first support (Arabic + English) with natural typography balance.

## Anti-References (What We MUST Avoid)
- Generic corporate SaaS dashboards (bright hex blue `#0284C7`, sharp dark modes with high contrast).
- High-saturation neon accents or noisy rainbow status indicators.
- Boxy nested cards with dark 1px borders and hard drop-shadows.
- Cluttered, text-heavy forms without clear visual rhythm.
